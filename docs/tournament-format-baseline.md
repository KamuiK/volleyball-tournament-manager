## Tournament format: 10-team baseline
- Two groups, A and B, of 5 teams each.
- Single round robin within each group; 4 group matches per team.
- First 4 teams in each group qualify for the quarterfinals; the two fifth-placed teams are eliminated.
- 20 group matches and 7 knockout matches: 27 total.
- Optional third-place match: 28 total.
- Minimum 4 matches per team; finalists play 7 matches.

## Group-stage scoring
- Exactly 2 fully played sets to 21 points.
- No two-point advantage; at 20:20, the next rally decides the set at 21:20.
- Match outcomes: 2:0, 1:1, or 0:2. No deciding set.
- Standings points: win 2; draw 1 per team; loss 0.
- Standings points = 2 × wins + draws.

### Group ranking, in order
1. Higher standings points.
2. Better rally-point difference across all group matches.
3. Higher total rally points scored.
4. Head-to-head if exactly two teams remain tied.
5. Drawing lots if still tied.

Rally-point difference = rally points scored minus rally points conceded across all group-stage sets.
Table columns: rank, team, matches played, wins, draws, losses, rally points for, rally points against, difference, standings points.

## Knockout scoring
- First two sets to 21 points.
- Two-point advantage required, with a hard cap at 23; 23:22 is valid.
- A team winning both sets wins the match 2:0.
- At 1:1, play a deciding set to 11.
- Deciding set: no two-point advantage; at 10:10, the next rally decides the set at 11:10.
- No draws; winner's result is 2:0 or 2:1.
